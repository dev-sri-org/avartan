Avartan · How to use

Avartan lays a taal cycle over any recording (a tabla theka, a lehra, or a performance) so you can see where you are in the cycle, know when sam and the bandish are coming, and practise at your own tempo.

1 · Get started

  1. Choose a recording. Pick one of the stock tracks from the Track list, or use your own: click 📂 Your own… (or "Open a file…" in the list), or drag an mp3 / wav / m4a / ogg / flac file onto the page. Stock tracks may come with the taal already set; the last stock track you used opens automatically next time.
  2. Pick the taal from the Taal list: Teentaal, Ektaal, Vilambit Ektaal, Jhaptaal, Rupak, and others. For anything else, choose Custom… (see below).
  3. Set the Original BPM, the tempo the recording was played at (matras per minute). If you don't know it, play the audio and tap Tap (or press T) on every matra. After 3 taps the BPM is filled in.
  4. Mark sam. Play the recording and press Mark sam (or S) right on the first sam. That moment becomes avartan 1, matra 1. You can also type the time in seconds in Avartan 1 sam at (s).
  5. Press Play (Space). The matra boxes, countdowns and beat view now follow the music.

2 · Fine-tune the alignment

If the grid slowly drifts away from the music, the BPM is slightly off. If it is off by a constant amount, sam is slightly off.

  • - / =: lower or raise the original BPM by 0.1 to fix drift.
  • [ / ]: move sam 10 ms earlier or later.
  • R: re-sync. Press it right on a sam heard in the music; the nearest sam snaps to that moment without changing the avartan numbering. Handy when a long recording drifts.
Settings are remembered per audio file. Open the same file again and your taal, BPM, sam and bandish come back.

3 · Practise slower (or faster)

Set Target BPM to the tempo you want to practise at. Playback speed = target ÷ original (shown next to it, e.g. 0.75×). The beat grid scales with it, so everything stays aligned. ↺ goes back to the original tempo.

4 · Bandish and tihais

Choose the matra your bandish (composition) starts on in Starts on matra. Avartan then:

  • marks that matra with a B in the matra boxes,
  • shows a Bandish in N countdown,
  • draws a Bandish flag in every avartan of the beat view, and
  • shows where a 3-matra tihai (3 × 3 matras) must start so that it lands on sam or on the bandish matra.

⇤ Bandish (B) jumps back to the latest bandish start; press it again to go one more avartan back.

5 · Reading the display

Matra boxesOne box per matra, grouped by vibhag, each showing its tali mark, bol and number. X = sam, 0 = khali (blue shading). The current matra is lit; the next one fills up as it approaches. The small bars at the bottom show the divisions. Click a box to jump to that matra.
Status lineAvartan number, taal, current tempo, the big current matra and bol, and the Bandish in / Sam in countdowns (they glow in the last 3 matras). The panel flashes on every sam.
Beat viewA scrolling timeline; the white line is the playhead. Strips from top to bottom: MATRA (numbers, marks, bols) · LAYA (one block per division, current lit) · BANDISH · 3-MATRA SAM TIHAI · 3-MATRA BANDISH TIHAI · AUDIO waveform. Click or drag on it to seek.
OverviewThe whole track, with sam ticks in red and bandish lines in green. The box shows the part visible above. Click or drag to seek anywhere.

6 · Player controls

  • ⏮ / ⏭: previous / next avartan (Shift+←/→). ←/→ move 2 s.
  • ⟳ Repeat (P): play the whole track over and over.
  • Loop av (L): loop the current avartan, or 2 or 4 avartans (chosen in the list next to it), from sam to sam.
  • Divisions: split each matra into 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 parts (dugun, tigun, chaugun…) in the boxes and the beat view.
  • Click: a metronome on Sam only, on every Vibhag, or on All matras. Sam, khali and tali have different pitches.
  • View: how many avartans fit in the beat view (½ to 4).
  • Matras (M) / Waveform (W): show or hide the matra boxes or the audio strip.
  • 🔊 Volume (↑/↓).

7 · Custom taal

Choose Custom… and fill in three fields (picking Custom right after a preset copies that preset so you can tweak it):

  • Vibhags: matras per vibhag, e.g. 3+2+2 for 7 matras.
  • Marks: one per vibhag, X for sam, 0 for khali, numbers for tali, e.g. X 2 3.
  • Bols: one per matra, separated by spaces.
